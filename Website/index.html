How to add this listing to your VCC

The quickest way is the Add to VCC button. To add the listing by hand, do the following:

  1. Open your VCC and go to Settings
  2. Click the Packages tab
  3. Click Add Repository
  4. Paste the URL below into the field that appears
  5. Click Add
  6. Check the repository info and click I Understand
  7. Open any of your projects to see the packages from the newly added listing
